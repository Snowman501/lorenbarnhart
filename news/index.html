Turn Around

One decision on Highway 71. One word from Stacy. Twenty-seven years of marriage.

In this personal essay, I share the afternoon I turned my 1987 Chevrolet C10 around—and met the woman who would become my wife. It is a story about survival, hope, and a future I could not have imagined that day.

Thank you to editor Jeff Wick and The Fayette County Record for treating our story with care and sharing it with their readers.
