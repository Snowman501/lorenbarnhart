She Said “Hi.”

Sometimes the rest of your life begins with one word.

By Loren Barnhart

That Valentine’s Day, I was driving my 1987 Chevrolet C10 on Highway 71 between Smithville and La Grange, Texas. A three-year relationship had ended. I thought I was headed toward the worst day of my life.

Then I turned the truck around. I went to Brandy, and she brought Stacy to me.

Stacy looked at me and said one word.

“Hi.”

That was it. One word. And my whole life turned on it.

The next day, I came back expecting her to have changed her mind. Instead, she greeted me with a smile and an open door. We married on September 10, 1999.

We have lived real years since then: love, hard seasons, and the work of finding our way back to each other. I’m an author now, and the stories I write are possible in part because Stacy believed I could keep going.

Years later, I spent two months in Florida with FEMA after a hurricane, away from Stacy. That separation was one of the hard times in my life. It reminded me how much being together means.

That first “Hi” still matters to me. So does every day we have chosen each other since.

— Loren
