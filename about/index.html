About the author

Loren Barnhart

A writer drawn to the things people carry—and the mysteries they cannot leave alone.

Stylized author artwork

From the shop to the page

Loren lives in Hot Springs Village, Arkansas. He spent more than twenty years as a dealer trained ASE Certified Master Technician, listening closely, following clues, and asking what everyone else might have missed. That habit of looking beneath the surface followed him into writing.

In his paranormal fiction, an ordinary place can hide an impossible truth. In his memoirs, he writes about the quieter mysteries: how a person survives difficult years, finds connection, and begins to speak honestly about what happened.

Why I write

Some stories start with a question you cannot put down. Others start with something you have carried for years.

I write because the strange and the deeply human belong on the same page. A haunting can ask what it means to remain yourself. A life story can ask what it takes to keep going, to love someone, and to choose yourself when you need to.

Whether you came for a mystery or a memoir, I hope you find a story that stays with you after the last page.
