About the author

Loren Barnhart

A writer drawn to the things people carry—and the mysteries they cannot leave alone.

Stylized author artwork

Loren lives in Hot Springs Village, Arkansas. After two decades as a dealer trained ASE Certified Master Technician, he brings a diagnostic eye to paranormal thrillers, while his memoirs explore survival, connection, and family.

His fiction includes San Pedro Protocol and The Relic Web. His nonfiction includes The Quiet Collapse and The Weight of Silence.
