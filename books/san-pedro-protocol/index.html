San Pedro Protocol

The house was only the beginning.

At a deserted bungalow in San Pedro, investigator Mark Chen finds evidence that a notorious haunting may have been connected to something far larger. Sarah Vance can sense what the house is transmitting. Since the night her human body died, she has lived inside a synthetic one—and the signal seems to recognize her.

Beyond the bungalow

Their search leads from the California coast to a hidden network of relays and, finally, a sealed facility beneath Highway 71 in Texas. Silas Clobery knows who built the network. He also knows what could happen if Mark and Sarah shut it down.
