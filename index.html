What The Relic Web is about

Mark Chen investigates things other people file and forget. Sarah Vance was built to survive what he investigates. Between them sits a chain of haunted sites — a shuttered facility in San Pedro, a research station buried in Arctic ice, a bunker called Providence-Zero — and a pattern nobody was ever meant to connect.

The relics aren't cursed. They're wired. And every one of them is drawing power from someone who used to be alive.

About Loren

I write paranormal sci-fi thrillers — the kind where the science is the haunting. Before this I spent years as an ASE Master Technician, which is mostly a job about finding the one broken thing inside a system that everybody swears is working fine. It turns out that's also what a thriller is.

I publish independently through SL NextGen Global, a two-person operation I run with my wife Stacy. Everything here is ours, start to finish.

The Fractured — a long-form horror mythology set in South Solon, Ohio — is what I'm writing next.
